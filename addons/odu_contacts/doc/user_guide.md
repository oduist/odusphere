# Contacts

The **Contacts** app is your everyday workspace for the people and companies you
deal with, and for the messages that arrive through your website's contact form.
It has two sections.

## Contacts

Open **Contacts → Contacts** to browse your directory of people and companies. You
can switch between the card (kanban), list and form views, search and filter, and
add or edit an entry like any other record. This is where you keep your
counterparties — clients, vendors, individual contacts.

## Requests

Open **Contacts → Requests** to handle messages sent from your website's contact
form. Every submission shows up here automatically. The list opens on the **Open**
requests (everything not yet finished).

Each request moves through three stages:

- **New** — just arrived, nobody has looked at it yet.
- **In Progress** — someone is working on it.
- **Done** — handled, nothing left to do.

You work a request like this:

- **Change the stage** by clicking the status bar at the top of the request, or by
  dragging its card between columns in the kanban view.
- **Assign an owner** with the *Assigned To* field, so it is clear who is
  responsible. Use the **My Requests** filter to see only what is assigned to you.
- **Discuss and keep a history** in the chatter at the bottom of the request: leave
  a note, log what you did, or follow the request to get notified of changes.
- **Schedule a follow-up** with an activity (for example "call back tomorrow"); due
  activities are highlighted in the list and on the card.

Use the **Group By → Status** or **Group By → Assigned To** options to get an
overview of the whole inbox at a glance.
